FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What does a free search show me?

The Viral Breakouts for your brand or product, ranked by Breakout Score, with the creator, views and likes for each video. Bookmark any of them and come back later.

What counts as one search?

One brand or product, plus any keywords you attach to it. Ticking six keywords still spends one search.

Why focus on breakouts?

A video with 4 million views from an account with 10 million followers is an ordinary day. The same views from an account with 4 thousand followers is a breakout. Breakout Score is weighted engagement relative to the creator's follower count.

Is the free search a trial?

No. Your account includes 3 free searches with no credit card. The 8-day trial on Growth or Scale is a separate step and needs a card.

What happens after the 8-day trial?

Unless you cancel before the trial ends, we charge your card for the plan and billing cycle you picked. Monthly plans bill monthly. Annual plans bill once a year.

How do I cancel my trial?

Go to Settings, then Subscription, and cancel before the trial ends.

Is the data real-time?

Close to it. New TikToks reach our index within hours of going live, and every video's Breakout Score keeps updating with its performance.
