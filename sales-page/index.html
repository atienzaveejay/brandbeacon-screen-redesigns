FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Still stuck? Chat with Beacon, our AI support assistant.

What does a free search show me?

The TikToks about your brand or product that broke out, ranked by Breakout Score, with views, likes and each creator's followers. Free also includes 5 video analyses, which show why a video worked and what to do next.

Why do I sign in before searching?

Every search pulls fresh TikToks and runs our analysis, so we ask for an email before the first one. It keeps free searches free for everyone.

What counts as one search?

One brand or one product. Add as many related keywords as you like and it still counts as one search.

Why focus on breakouts?

4 million views from a creator who usually gets 4 million is normal. 4 million views from a creator who usually gets 4 thousand means the video itself did something right. Those are breakouts, and they're the ones worth learning from.

Is the free search a trial?

No. You get 3 free searches, no credit card needed. The 8-day free trial of Growth or Scale is separate, and that one does need a card.

What happens after the 8-day trial?

If you don't cancel before the 8 days are up, we charge your card for the plan you picked, monthly or annual.

How do I cancel my trial?

Go to Settings, then Subscription, and cancel before the trial ends. You won't be charged.

Is the data real-time?

Each search pulls the latest TikToks when it runs, and saved searches refresh every week.
