  • Score: every outlier tool shows a multiple (7×) against the account's usual views. Viewstats uses the last 10 videos. 4Share this video

What changed and why

SHARE THIS VIDEO: what changed

Ivan, 18 Sep: share at video level only, to your team or creators. Push viewers to a free account.

  • NEW: 'Who is it for': My team or Creators.
  • NEW: Copy link, not send email. One link per creator so you see who opened it. The brand pastes it into its own DM, email or TikTok Shop invite. That avoids TikTok scraping rules and CAN-SPAM.
  • NEW: structured deal fields (pay, due by, product link, sample, Shop commission, Spark code). Insense and TikTok Shop Target Collaboration ask for these; creators want pay and usage upfront.
  • NEW: each step tagged MUST KEEP or YOUR CALL. Creator surveys say they want to know where they have freedom.

WHAT THE CREATOR OPENS: what changed

  • Brand name and site in the header. A no-login page has to show who is asking.
  • Deal box first: pay, due by, sample, Spark code.
  • Three answers: I'm interested / Ask a question / Not for me, so the brand sees who replied (TikTok One pattern).
  • NEW (Ivan): 'one of 55 breakouts' card pushing a free account.

BLOCKER: the site still serves the title 'Outlier Vault' with no og tags, so every shared link shows a blank card. Fix before this ships.

What changed and why

BB ADMIN DASHBOARD: what changed

From the admin pages saved on 19 Sep. Totals only, no customer names or emails. Numbers are the real totals from those pages. [N] means the page does not show it yet.

  • One funnel. Today the same range shows 8 and 33 sign ups, and 0, 4 and 12 paid in different cards.
