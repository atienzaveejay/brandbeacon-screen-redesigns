Sign in, three searches, three breakdowns

The flow, step by step

Ten steps after the 24 Sept notes: the analyze confirm is gone, and the share action and the page a share link opens are steps of their own. Click any image to open it full size.

01

Landing page, built around sign-in

  • Google is the primary button and the only solid element on the page. Email sits under it as the other way in.
  • The offer now reads sign up to get 3 brand or product searches, 3 AI breakdowns, no card, and has real space above it so it no longer crowds the subhead.
  • The dark numbers band is gone. Three of its four figures did not hold up: the refresh is not a global Monday, the breakout count is not guaranteed, and the top score changes every cycle. Rather than invent replacements it came out, and the refresh cycle is now explained in the questions instead.
  • Under the fold: how the three searches work, a full sample breakdown, Free vs Growth, and four questions. The dashed line marks where the fold falls.

Ivan’s line “Not enough space between sign up to get and the sub title above” and “the dark brown bar … does not make sense, consider removing.”

02

Search a brand or a product

  • “Narrow it down” is gone. It was our reading of “(expand)” in your first list and it did not land, so the screen is back to one input.
  • Products are first class now. The headline asks for either, and the suggestion chips are mixed and labelled: a tag icon for brands, a target for products.
  • Brand still leads, as you asked: it is named first everywhere and a brand search returns everything posted about it, where a product search returns that line plus the brand behind it.

Ivan’s line “It’s not just brand searches, it’s brand and products, both important, brand slightly more” and “i dont understand narrow it down.”

03

Processing, with the welcome video over it

  • The estimate is a counting-down timer rather than the words “about 1 min left”.
  • No count is promised. The step reads “scoring every breakout we find”, since the number is whatever the pull returns.
  • The video slot is unchanged and works for either you on camera or an AI voiceover.

Ivan’s line “It’s not necessarily 200 breakouts, no guaranteed” and “about 1 min left should be a timer.”

04

Search results

  • Yes, this is close to what exists today. The list, the ranking and the scores are the current page. Three things differ: Analyze sits on the top breakout instead of every card, Export is in the header, and the count claim is gone from the subhead.
  • If the live page is already doing all three, this screen needs no build at all.
  • Nothing is masked or blurred, because by this point the person is signed in.

Ivan’s line “Will be the same with what we have today?”

05

Breakdown, with Share

  • The confirm step before this is deleted. Picking a video runs the breakdown the way the live app does now.
  • This is the existing breakdown page with a Share button added, as you said, plus the Do this next block and creator line from the 23 Sept round. Say the word if those should come out and it goes back to the page as it stands.
  • Share is the primary action and is free on every plan.

Ivan’s line “I dont think we need this [the analyze prompt], we’ll use the same as existing” and “the breakdown page, we’ll just add a share button to what we have already.”

06

What happens when Share is pressed

  • New screen. The link is generated and shown ready to copy, with a preview of the card that will appear when it is pasted.
  • It states plainly that the recipient needs no account and that sharing does not spend one of the three.
  • Copy link, email, Slack and a downloadable image. On mobile it is a bottom sheet.

Ivan’s line “Also need to show what will happen after the user selects to share in page 6.”

07

The page a share link opens

  • Built out properly. It was a summary; it now carries the whole video: the embed, the five engagement figures, the creator and when they posted, and all four drivers rather than three.
  • The Breakout Score is explained on the page, with the baseline it is measured against, because most people arriving here have never seen the number before.
  • This is the only screen in the flow with no account behind it, so it is the one front door a stranger meets.

Ivan’s line “I feel the share receive page is not complete? Is this the whole video details?”

08

Prompt to search another brand

  • When it shows up: on the way back from a breakdown, the first time someone returns to search with credits left. Not on a timer and not on every visit, or it becomes nagging.
  • It suggests competitors of the brand just searched, which is the reason a brand team runs a second search at all.
  • If that trigger is wrong, the alternative is to fold it into the results page as a quiet strip rather than a full screen.

Ivan’s line “When will 7 show up?”

09

Paywall on the fourth search

  • The wall names what is still free beside what is blocked: the brands already searched, the breakdowns already run, and every share link already sent.
  • Nothing is deleted and nothing expires. The block is on new searches only.
  • One plan, one price, one button.

Ivan’s line Unchanged from the 24 Sept build.

10

Paywall on the fourth breakdown

  • Same wall, different trigger, and it keeps the video in view so it is clear what was being asked for.
  • The score and the video stay free. Only the written breakdown is behind the wall.
  • Growth is quoted as 100 breakdowns a month against the 3 just spent.

Ivan’s line Unchanged from the 24 Sept build.

What we saw first-hand

Walked through in a browser on 22 Sept 2026, logged out and then with a trial account. Screenshots are cropped to remove account details.

Brand Beacon today

Walked, free account
  • You type a brand and pick keywords, then “Run my free search” goes straight to Google sign-in. Email sign-up exists at /register but is not on the main buttons.
  • The popup promises “Your free top-video breakdown stays included”, but Free has 0 analyses.
  • No Share button on a report or a video yet.
  • The 8-day trial collects a card up front.

Kalodata

Walked, trial accountkalodata.com
  • Visitors browse the actual app with every number shown as “$****”.
  • 7-day trial, no card, 15 AI credits. A “3 reasons this video went viral” report took about 3 minutes and 2.75 credits.
What we take: show the real list and hide the valuable part; let the upgrade prompt appear mid-answer without blocking it.

FastMoss

Walked, logged outfastmoss.com
  • Logged-out search returns real data in the dropdown, and the dashboard is public.
  • Shop pages show the headline stats and one row, then “Unlock all 137 products, sign up free”.
What we take: a taste of real data before any account, with a clear count of what is behind the wall.

SparkToro

Walked, free accountsparktoro.com
  • You describe your audience first; sign-up comes second and the query carries through.
  • Report ready in about 5 seconds. 3 free reports a month, no card.
What we take: the founder welcome video (no one else did this), and locked premium sections shown after real results.

Exolyt

Walked, trial accountexolyt.com
  • Two forms, about 9 questions, before anything shows.
  • Then the full top plan for 7 days, no card: “After 7 days, we’ll automatically switch you to our free plan.”
  • Its “Brand Mentions” tab lists accounts the brand itself tags. It does not find creators posting about the brand or score them against their baseline.
What we take: the reverse trial works in this category. Our no-survey start is an advantage worth keeping.

Other apps that shaped it

Walked logged out on 22 Sept, except where marked “from published sources”.

Loom

From published sources

Sign up → record → share → someone views it (the aha) → trial offer

Its data showed the aha was the first view of a shared video, not the recording. Viewers watch with no account; the upgrade offer comes after the first success.

Calendly

From published sources

Sign up → ready-made event and link → invitee books → invitee sees “Powered by Calendly”

A working default instead of an empty screen, and a badge on every shared link.

Gamma

From published sources

Sign up → prompt → AI deck in about a minute → share with a “Made with Gamma” badge

A growth teardown reports most users dropped off on a blank canvas before seeing AI output; getting the output to them fast fixed it.

remove.bg

From published sources

Upload with no account → result in seconds → sign up only for HD

The full result is free to see; only the high-value export is paid.

Similarweb

From published sources

Type a domain → basic report, no account → depth and history paid

The closest shape to Brand Beacon’s search. Its site blocks automated browsers, so we did not walk it.

Senja

From published sources

Sign up → import testimonials you already have → your own wall in minutes

The aha comes from the user’s own data. For us: your brand’s top breakout and why it worked.
