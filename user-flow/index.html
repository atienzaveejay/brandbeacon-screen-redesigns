The flow, step by step

Eight steps, desktop and mobile. Each note lists the apps that inspired the step. Click any image to open it full size.

01

Homepage: type a brand, no account

  • Search is the first thing on the page, with an example breakdown beside it so visitors see what they will get.
  • Trust line under the search: preview before you sign up, no card, 11,000+ brands tracked weekly.

Inspired by Plausible (live demo on the homepage), Ahrefs (the tool is the landing page), ChatGPT (the input box is the homepage).

02

Preview: real videos, scores hidden

  • A typed brand shows its real breakout videos straight away, with Breakout Scores and the AI breakdown locked.
  • The four "why it broke out" driver titles are visible; the detail unlocks with a free account.

Inspired by Kalodata (whole app visible logged out, numbers masked), FastMoss (first row real, the rest blurred), Exploding Topics (chart free, deep analysis blurred).

03

Not indexed yet: sign up and we build it

  • If a brand is not in the index, sign-up starts the build (about 2 minutes) and the search is saved.
  • Real breakouts from the category fill the page meanwhile, so it is never empty.

Inspired by SparkToro (you type first, sign up second, and the query carries through).

04

Sign up to reveal, no card

  • Sign-up is framed as the reveal: scores for all 200 videos, one free AI breakdown, weekly tracking, 8 days of Growth.
  • Google and email side by side. Email sign-up already works at /register; it just is not on the main buttons today.

Inspired by Kalodata, FastMoss and Exolyt all run no-card trials. Tally asks for an account only at the moment of Publish.

05

Welcome video from Ivan while it builds

  • A 90 second welcome from Ivan plays during the 1 to 2 minute build: how to read a Breakout Score and what to do with the first breakout.
  • Beside it: build progress, and the trial terms in one card.

Inspired by SparkToro (co-founder welcome video shown while the first report loads). None of the other tools did this.

06

The aha: a free breakdown, then Share

  • The report opens on its top breakout, already analyzed and labeled "Your free breakdown".
  • Share is the main button. A second analysis offers Growth but never blocks the answer.

Inspired by Kalodata (a day-one trial user got a full "why it went viral" report; the upgrade prompt appears mid-answer with "continue with current benefits"), Loom (upgrade offer comes after the first success).

07

Public shared page

  • A shared breakdown opens with no account: TikTok embed, Brand Beacon's analysis, credit to the creator, and a button into the @rhode preview.
  • This is the second front door, and most people will open it on a phone.

Inspired by Loom (viewers watch free, sign-up comes on an action), Calendly, Gamma and Tally (every shared output carries the product's name).

08

My Feed: 1 brand tracked free, weekly

  • After the trial the account drops to Free and keeps tracking one brand every Monday, so there is a reason to come back.
  • Upgrade prompts are about tracking more brands, not about seeing anything at all.

Inspired by Exolyt (reverse trial: "we'll automatically switch you to our free plan"), Ahrefs (free tools lead to "track your own site").

What we saw first-hand

Walked through in a browser on 22 Sept 2026, logged out and then with a trial account. Screenshots are cropped to remove account details.

Brand Beacon today

Walked, free account
  • You type a brand and pick keywords, then “Run my free search” goes straight to Google sign-in. Email sign-up exists at /register but is not on the main buttons.
  • The popup promises “Your free top-video breakdown stays included”, but Free has 0 analyses.
  • No Share button on a report or a video yet.
  • The 8-day trial collects a card up front.

Kalodata

Walked, trial accountkalodata.com
  • Visitors browse the actual app with every number shown as “$****”.
  • 7-day trial, no card, 15 AI credits. A “3 reasons this video went viral” report took about 3 minutes and 2.75 credits.
What we take: show the real list and hide the valuable part; let the upgrade prompt appear mid-answer without blocking it.

FastMoss

Walked, logged outfastmoss.com
  • Logged-out search returns real data in the dropdown, and the dashboard is public.
  • Shop pages show the headline stats and one row, then “Unlock all 137 products, sign up free”.
What we take: a taste of real data before any account, with a clear count of what is behind the wall.

SparkToro

Walked, free accountsparktoro.com
  • You describe your audience first; sign-up comes second and the query carries through.
  • Report ready in about 5 seconds. 3 free reports a month, no card.
What we take: the founder welcome video (no one else did this), and locked premium sections shown after real results.

Exolyt

Walked, trial accountexolyt.com
  • Two forms, about 9 questions, before anything shows.
  • Then the full top plan for 7 days, no card: “After 7 days, we’ll automatically switch you to our free plan.”
  • Its “Brand Mentions” tab lists accounts the brand itself tags. It does not find creators posting about the brand or score them against their baseline.
What we take: the reverse trial works in this category. Our no-survey start is an advantage worth keeping.

Other apps that shaped it

Walked logged out on 22 Sept, except where marked “from published sources”.

Loom

From published sources

Sign up → record → share → someone views it (the aha) → trial offer

Its data showed the aha was the first view of a shared video, not the recording. Viewers watch with no account; the upgrade offer comes after the first success.

Calendly

From published sources

Sign up → ready-made event and link → invitee books → invitee sees “Powered by Calendly”

A working default instead of an empty screen, and a badge on every shared link.

Gamma

From published sources

Sign up → prompt → AI deck in about a minute → share with a “Made with Gamma” badge

A growth teardown reports most users dropped off on a blank canvas before seeing AI output; getting the output to them fast fixed it.

remove.bg

From published sources

Upload with no account → result in seconds → sign up only for HD

The full result is free to see; only the high-value export is paid.

Similarweb

From published sources

Type a domain → basic report, no account → depth and history paid

The closest shape to Brand Beacon’s search. Its site blocks automated browsers, so we did not walk it.

Senja

From published sources

Sign up → import testimonials you already have → your own wall in minutes

The aha comes from the user’s own data. For us: your brand’s top breakout and why it worked.
