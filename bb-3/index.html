1One search for brands and products

A matching account opens as its brand card, with the keyword breakouts underneath. One search credit.

2Smarter keyword suggestions (dev note)

For Lester: Brand or account term: suggest its own product lines and drop matches that only share the word (rhode skin keeps lip tint and glazing milk, drops rhode jewelry). Product or category term: suggest close variants and synonyms, plus Similar brands, the brands that appear most in breakouts for that category. Build the list from the term plus the captions and hashtags of the top TikTok results, up to 6 keywords and 4 brands, with the 2 strongest ticked. Suggest different keywords reruns it without the ones already shown. Hashtag suggestions stay on Home (row 1).

3Results page, simplified

Three quiet numbers, one line of insight, then the videos. Average Breakout Score, the Breakout Score definition, Alert me and Share all stay.

4Left sidebar

Recent searches, Processing only while something runs, My Library, and a smaller usage meter, with the same spacing between groups.

5Home: your searches plus new breakouts, My Feed in the left bar

Home is Your searches plus one row of four new breakouts. The A and B layouts from 11 Oct are retired.

Free includes 3 searches and 5 video analyses (agreed on Slack), so every new account can finish all four steps.
