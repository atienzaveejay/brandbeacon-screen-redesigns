Navigation

1Left sidebarOpen in prototype

Live today: My Feed, Brand searches, Product searches, Library, Breakdowns.

  1. Order: Home, My Feed, Processing, Recent searches, My Library. Usage and account sit at the bottom.
  2. Home is the new default page after login. My Feed moves directly under it.
  3. Processing shows only while a search or a video analysis runs. One row per job: name, type (Search or Analysis), progress bar. No jobs, no group.
  4. When a job finishes it leaves Processing and a toast says {term} is ready with Open. A finished search goes to the top of Recent searches with a Ready pill until opened.
  5. Recent searches replaces Brand searches and Product searches. Last 5 by run date, then See more (opens Search history). Pill N new when a refresh found breakouts since the last visit.
  6. My Library replaces Library: Saved videos and Video analyses, with counts.
  7. Usage: two thin lines, Searches and Breakdowns, shown as used of total. Plan and reset date go under the name.
  8. Rows are tighter than live. Keep the space between groups.
  9. Phone: the same contents in a drawer. See Phone rules.

Home and search

2HomeOpen in prototype

Live today: Lands on My Feed: a Brand / Product toggle, search, then a long video feed.

  1. Search card on top: see search and keyword step.
  2. Your searches: the user's searches as a plain list. Row: avatar, name, BRAND or PRODUCT tag, N breakouts · top score · next refresh, N new pill. Click opens the results. Search history link on the right.
  3. New in your searches: one row of the 4 newest breakouts across the user's searches, newest first. Card: thumbnail, Breakout Score, handle, search · age. Click opens that search's results. See all in My Feed.
  4. OURSNo searches yet: show the search card only. No new breakouts: hide that row.
  5. Phone: same blocks. Find breakouts sits full width under the box, as live. New in your searches goes 2 across.

3One search box, with suggestionsOpen in prototype

Live today: Brand / Product toggle above the box. One row of Suggested brands to track.

  1. Remove the Brand / Product toggle. One box: Search a brand, product or keyword.
  2. Dropdown while typing: a matching TikTok account first, then keywords. AS LIVE
  3. Enter, Find breakouts or a dropdown pick opens the keyword step. A matching account runs as its brand, with the keyword breakouts underneath. One search credit per run.
  4. Three suggestion rows under the box: Brands, Products, Hashtags, 5 chips each. A chip fills the box and opens the keyword step.
  5. A hashtag chip runs as its keyword: #glazedskin runs glazed skin. Search stays keywords only.
  6. SAMPLEChip lists in the mock are placeholders.
  7. OURSPhone: each suggestion row is one line that scrolls sideways.

4Keyword step: smarter suggestionsOpen in prototype

Live today: Same panel. rhode still suggests rhode jewelry, earrings and necklace.

  1. Panel stays as live: Add keywords, Suggest different keywords, Add a keyword, Cancel, Run search.
  2. Brand or account term: suggest its own product lines. Drop matches that only share the word (rhode keeps lip tint and glazing milk, drops rhode jewelry).
  3. Product or category term: suggest close variants and synonyms.
  4. New row, Similar brands: the brands that show up most in breakouts for that category. Ticking one adds it as a keyword.
  5. Build the list from the term plus the captions and hashtags of the top TikTok results. Up to 6 keywords and 4 brands. The 2 strongest come ticked.
  6. Suggest different keywords reruns it without the ones already shown.
  7. Run search starts the search. The first search goes to wait and learn, every later one back to Home.
  8. OURSPhone: Cancel and Run search split the width.

Running a search

5First search: wait and learn (A)Open in prototype

Live today: A loading page with nothing to do while the report builds.

  1. Only the account's first search lands here. Every later search goes back to Home.
  2. Title Finding {term} breakouts. Line: Usually 1 to 2 minutes. You can leave this page. SAMPLEthe 1 to 2 minutes is from the 22 Sep test.
  3. Three steps tick as the job moves: pulling TikTok videos, comparing each to the creator's usual views, picking the breakouts. Map them to the real pipeline stages.
  4. Email me when it's ready: on by default. If they leave, the first search email goes out. Off means no email.
  5. How to read your results: 1:30 video from Ivan. Placeholder in the mock.
  6. Breakouts found this week: 3 recent breakouts from other searches, live card design. Analyze and save work here.
  7. Sidebar: the search sits under Processing. Get started shows step 1 spinning. Searches reads 1 of 3 from the moment it starts.
  8. On completion the page turns into the Ready state in place.

6Later searches: back to Home (B)Open in prototype

Live today: Same loading page for every search.

  1. Every search after the first: Run search goes straight back to Home.
  2. One running line on top of Home: {term} is running. About 1 to 2 minutes. We'll tell you here and by email.
  3. See progress opens the wait page for that search (wait and learn layout).
  4. The search shows under Processing, next to any video analysis that is running.
  5. They can keep browsing, analyze videos or start another search.
  6. OURSPhone: a spinner chip with the job count sits in the top bar. Tap opens the drawer with Processing.

New accounts

12Get started and Free limitsOpen in prototype

Live today: New accounts land on My Feed with no next step. Free is 1 search and 0 video analyses.

  1. New Free accounts see Get started in the left column, above usage. 4 steps: Run your first search, Analyze a video, Save a video, Run a second search.
  2. Each step ticks when its action happens. Counter N of 4.
  3. The block goes away once all 4 are done. Paid accounts never see it.
  4. Free = 3 searches and 5 video analyses (live: 1 and 0). Usage lines read 1 of 3 and 0 of 5. Paywall on the 4th search or the 6th analysis.
  5. Home shows only the account's own searches.
  6. IVAN 12 OCTPhone: the 4 steps sit as a card at the top of Home, with the same ticks and count as the left column. The drawer leaves them out on phone.

Email spec

  • Trigger: the account's first search_completed. Once per account.
  • Skip if they already opened the results, or turned off Email me when it's ready.
  • From: Ivan from BrandBeacon. Subject: Your {{search_term}} breakouts are ready. Preview: {{breakout_count}} TikTok videos beat their creator's usual views.
  • Body as shown. One image (the top video's still), one button See your breakouts to results_url, signed Ivan.
  • Merge tags: first_name, search_term, breakout_count, top_score, top_video_thumb, results_url.
  • Replaces free flow email B1 Results ready. If B1 is live, this is a copy change.
